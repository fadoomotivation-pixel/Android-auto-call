# Test it like a real telecaller would — 10-step checklist

Do this **on a real Android phone, with a real SIM**, acting exactly like a telecaller on
their first day. One full run tells you more than a week of demos. ~30–45 minutes.

## Before you start (5 min)
- [ ] A real Android phone with a working SIM (ideally a common Indian brand — Xiaomi/Redmi, Vivo, Oppo, Samsung — because those are what your customers' reps will use).
- [ ] Install the latest app (from the repo's **Releases → android-latest**).
- [ ] Have a **second phone/number** handy to act as the "customer" — so you can see both sides.
- [ ] (For WhatsApp steps) the company WhatsApp number connected in Admin → 💬 WhatsApp. If not connected yet, skip steps 8 and note it.

## The 10 steps
1. **Log in** as a telecaller account. → Should stay logged in, land on the dashboard.
2. **Punch in attendance** (selfie + location). → Should save and show "on shift."
3. **Open the Leads tab.** → Leads load, cards look clean/premium, your name/count up top. Tap **AI Score** — leads get ranked.
4. **Tap 📞 Call on a lead** (use your second number as the lead). → The phone places a real call. Talk for ~20 seconds, then hang up.
5. **Open the Calls tab.** → That call should appear **by itself** with the right number and duration. *(This is the make-or-break feature — calls must log without you doing anything.)*
6. **Wait ~1 minute, refresh.** → A **recording** + an **AI summary** of the call should appear. *(If recording doesn't work, note your exact phone model — recording is the most phone-dependent part.)*
7. **Apply the AI-suggested status** (e.g. "Interested") or set one manually. → The lead's stage updates.
8. **Open WhatsApp on a lead → send a message** from inside the app. → It sends, and shows up in the chat. *(First message to a new number may need an approved template — that's a WhatsApp rule, not a bug.)*
9. **Schedule a follow-up** — pick a date & time. → It shows in the Follow-up calendar; you get a reminder at that time.
10. **Switch to the Admin web dashboard (as the boss).** → You should see that rep's **call, recording, AI summary, WhatsApp chat, and attendance** — all of it. *(This is your whole pitch: total visibility.)*

## What should make you nervous (watch for these)
- **Step 5 fails after the phone sits idle / screen off for a while** → the OEM "killed" the app in the background. The reconciliation sync should backfill it when you reopen the app — confirm it does. This is the #1 real-world risk in India.
- **Step 6 has no recording** → call recording is blocked on some phone models / Android versions. Know which models work before you promise it to a client.
- **Step 8 won't send the first message** → you need a Meta-approved WhatsApp template for first-contact. Normal — just plan for it.

## Pass bar before selling to a paying client
✅ Steps 1–5 and 10 work **reliably, 5 times in a row, including after the phone has been locked for 30 minutes.**
Calls logging + boss visibility are the promise. Everything else is a bonus.
